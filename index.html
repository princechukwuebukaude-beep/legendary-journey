In the Spotlight

Chukwuebuka Ude is a distinguished officer in the Nigerian Air Force, where discipline, honor, and service define his daily life. Outside the military, he is a devoted husband and father of three, a gospel minister, a visionary designer, and an inspirational writer. He is also the founder and CEO of WealthCrown Enterprise and Ventures — a purpose-driven company rooted in faith, creativity, and transformative impact.

My Mission

Central to everything I do is my faith in Jesus Christ. my life is a testimony of grace. Once lost, but now am found by Jesus Christ, who saved me from sin and gave me a new identity. Since then, I’ve made it my mission to preach the gospel wherever I go through my words, my songs, and the stories I write. I believe that every life can be transformed, and I use my gifts to share that message with the world. Through my designs, I express beauty, purpose, and divine inspiration. Through my books and songs, I speak life into hearts that need healing. Whether I’m sketching a new concept, writing a chapter, or lifting my voice in worship, I do it all to glorify God and uplift others.
